# French Data Library attribution and handling

I use daily value-weighted U.S. industry portfolio returns from the [Kenneth R. French Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html). The primary panel is the daily 10-industry series, retrieved 5 November 2026. The post-freeze aggregation check uses the daily 17-industry series, retrieved 6 November 2026.

I provide download scripts and record the source URL, retrieval time, and SHA-256 hashes. The repository does not contain the raw archives or extracted source rows, and the CC BY 4.0 notice does not apply to them. The French Library's own terms govern the source data. The analysis outputs I derive from those data are covered by `LICENSE-PAPER.md`.
